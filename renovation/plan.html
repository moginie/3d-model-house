21 Merino Street — proposed floor plan (for approval)

Existing on the left, proposed on the right. North is up, street at the bottom. Sizes are internal, wall to wall.

Existing walls kept New walls / extension Windows (enlarged) New feature fence Colorbond fence Cross-breeze path

Key moves

  • Side walls out: west +0.5 m, east +0.75 m. House goes from 15.7 m to 16.9 m wide and keeps 1.5 m clear each side (the Qld minimum side setback), so both side paths stay usable.
  • Front flush: lounge wall and front door move out to line up with the bedroom walls. The old porch is already under the main roof, so this is cheap floor area.
  • Sunroom removed: replaced by a master suite (bedroom, walk-in robe, ensuite) and a covered 5.0 × 4.0 m deck off the kitchen, facing north for sun.
  • Open plan: fridge and pantry walls go, so kitchen, dining and living become one 5.0 × 6.9 m room with an island and a 2.7 m stacking door to the deck.
  • Wet rooms stay where the plumbing already runs (north side of the east wing). The laundry moves to the east side wall, with an outside door, and all services sit on the east side path.
  • Two existing walls on each side of the living area stay, which limits structural work.

Proposed rooms

Windows and mould

  • Bigger windows: 3.1 m living window, 1.8–2.4 m bedroom windows, 2.7 m stacking door, glazed door from the master to the deck.
  • Cross-breezes: front window to deck doors, hall to a new east door, corner windows in Bed 3 and Bed 5.
  • Exhaust fans in the bath, ensuite, WC and laundry, plus a rangehood, all ducted outside (not into the roof space). This is the main mould fix.
  • Louvre or awning windows in wet rooms, opening skylights in the kitchen and hall, and ceiling fans in all bedrooms and living.
  • Roof space: two whirlybirds, eave vents, R4 ceiling insulation and anti-condensation sarking.
  • Dryer vented outside, and surface drains along both side paths so water doesn't sit against the walls.

Carport, fence and shed

  • Carport: rebuilt open-sided at 5.8 × 6.0 m, with a flat charcoal Colorbond roof tied to the house fascia, LED lights and a covered walk to a front portico.
  • Fence: 1.5 m horizontal aluminium slat fence (timber look) with rendered piers along the street and east front boundary. It has a pedestrian gate on a new front path, plus side gates at the building line on both sides. A sliding driveway gate is optional. The rear and side Colorbond fences stay.
  • Shed: moved to the rear north-east corner, close to the boundaries, which frees up the middle of the back yard. It's reached by the east side path, which is the "service side" with the laundry door, hot water, AC units, bins and clothesline.

Cost and build notes

  • The biggest costs are rebuilding the two side walls and the new roof, plus new drainage for the ensuite.
  • Cheaper option: skip the west extension. Bed 4 and Bed 5 would be 3.0 m wide instead of 3.5 m.
  • One new corrugated Colorbond gable roof (15° pitch) with a single ridge running the full width covers the house, suite, deck and a new paved patio behind the laundry. No hips or valleys, so it is the simplest roof to build and the least likely to leak. The side extensions mean the roof is replaced anyway. The deck sits on the old sunroom slab area.
  • The carport position on the front setback needs a council check, as the existing one does.
